Factory Infrastructure

In-House Manufacturing Since 1980

Four decades of precision metal engineering, continuous tooling investment, and cooling quality controls in Sultanpur.

Unlike traders or assembled vendors, Jai Hind Engineering maintains complete in-house fabrication machinery. From raw stainless steel sheet shearing, CNC hydraulic press bending, automated high-pressure PUF injection, to helium & nitrogen leak testing at 350 PSI, every step is executed under one factory roof.

We serve over 5,000+ businesses across Uttar Pradesh and North India — from prestigious heritage sweet shops and bakery chains to dairy cooperatives, hospital blood banks, and supermarket chains.

100% SS-304 Verified

Non-magnetic food-grade stainless steel tested with chemical spot analysis. Zero surface rusting.

350 PSI Leak Tested

Every refrigeration circuit undergoes 24-hour nitrogen pressure holding before gas charging.
